Chapter 1: Watch Out for Azrael!

  • Climb Birch Trees: Azrael can't climb smooth white birch bark, but you can!
  • Poke Sarsaparilla Leaves: Gargamel puts glue on bushes. Poke leaves with a stick first—if it sticks, run!
  • Hide Near Berries: Your blue skin blends right in with blueberries and bluebells!

Chapter 2: Berry & Emergency Rules

  • Count the Leaves: Safe Smurfberries have 5 green leaves. Bad berries only have 3 leaves!
  • Acorn Whistle: 3 quick whistle squeaks mean RED ALERT. Head to the subterranean tree tunnels!
  • If You Get Lost: Stay where you are and sing the La-La Song loudly so Papa Smurf can find you.
